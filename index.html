H(jω) is the complex voltage transfer function Vout⁄Vs. Magnitude is plotted as 20·log₁₀|H|, phase as the unwrapped angle of H in degrees. Resonant frequency and Q are both derived two ways: analytically from L and C, and numerically from the swept curve's peak and −3 dB bandwidth — the two will diverge once loss resistances or heavy loading add real damping.
